---
type: mission
id: TMISSION_BERZERK
title: Go Berzerk
summary: Does nothing at all, and holds the team on this line for the rest of the match.
caveats:
  - The line does nothing at all. Team execution has no handling for it, and there is no fallback for a line it cannot handle.
  - Nothing raises the team's advance either, so the team stays on this line for the rest of the match and never reaches the line after it. Put it at the end of a script and everything below it is unreachable.
  - It is the one team mission in the whole list that team execution has no handling for.
  - The trigger action of the same name does work. It sends every infantry carrying the trigger's tag berserk.
related:
  - type: system
    id: ai-team-execution
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_GO_BERZERK
---

A team that reaches this line is stopped there permanently. It keeps its members, keeps its orders from whatever line ran before, and answers damage as usual, but the [script step](/systems/ai-team-execution/#the-script-cursor) hands the line to nothing and nothing moves the team on. There is no way back out from inside the script; only being sent to regroup and started again — which restarts the script from the top — or being disbanded ends the wait.

[Go Berzerk](/mapping/actions/taction-go-berzerk/) as a trigger action is the working route to the same effect, and it aims at the infantry carrying a tag rather than at a team.
